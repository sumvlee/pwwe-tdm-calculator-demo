PWWE TDM-guided ASM dosing calculator

Independent sparse-TDM MAP updating with no absolute daily-dose ceiling. Risk-dependent dose steps and relative-increase rules are retained. All calculations run in your browser; inputs are not uploaded.

Inputs

Use the dose corresponding to the preconception target trough. The current measurement is included once in the MAP update.

Earlier TDM measurements
